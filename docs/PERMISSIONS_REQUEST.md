**Subject: PHerc. 1667 — permission and attribution for a noncommercial public reading project**

Dear research team,

Phoenix Scrolls is an independent educational website dedicated exclusively to the Herculaneum papyri. It is intended especially for readers without Greek or specialist archaeological training.

We would like to present the Greek transcription and published English translation of PHerc. 1667 side by side, with an optional, clearly distinguished modern-English interpretation. Missing or uncertain material would stay identifiable. Our pilot currently uses only a short attributed Greek quotation for discussion; it does not redistribute the complete English translation or images.

Could you clarify whether the data availability statement's CC BY-NC 4.0 terms also cover the complete Greek transcription and the English translation in your paper? If not, would you permit their reproduction on our free, noncommercial site and in its public GitHub repository?

We would also appreciate guidance on permission for interpretive adaptations, selected flattened papyrus renderings, any additional institutional restrictions, the required attribution, and the preferred citation/versioning procedure for later corrections. We will not imply institutional affiliation or endorsement.
